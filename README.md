# One Word From Your Ex

Make.fun entry for **One Word From Your Ex** — send exactly one word, the word your ex would use to describe you, and nothing else.

## The word

**Subscription**

Why this word: it is the label of someone the ex kept paying for every month, never really used, and somehow never got around to cancelling — auto-renewed, quietly draining, and only missed once the charges stopped. It is funny because a person reduced to a monthly bill is an absurd, instantly recognisable image; it stings because it is painfully honest (money and attention went out, nothing came back); and it is fresh — a consumer-economy metaphor, far from the crowded "almost / practice / unfinished" and "exhausting / forgettable" tropes. Anyone who has ever forgotten to cancel a subscription feels the recognition immediately.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the single word to `/output/entry.txt` (one word, 12 characters, letters only), after validating it is exactly one word within the 1–24 character gate and reading it back to confirm it is byte-identical.
